Detailed Description

Education-specific mark rules: validation, approval bands and approver selection.

Bands (default):

  • below 40 : no approver, the chain cannot approve -> Rejected
  • 40 to 49 : Moderator
  • 50 to 70 : TA (a different TA from the assignee)
  • 71 to 100 : Lecturer
