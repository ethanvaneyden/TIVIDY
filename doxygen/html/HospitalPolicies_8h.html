Detailed Description

Hospital-specific severity rules: validation, approval bands and approver selection.

Bands (agreed default):

  • 1 to 3 : Triage Nurse
  • 4 to 6 : Admitting Doctor
  • 7 to 10 : Chief of Medicine
  • anything else (0, negative, above 10) : no approver, the chain cannot approve -> the treatment is Rejected
