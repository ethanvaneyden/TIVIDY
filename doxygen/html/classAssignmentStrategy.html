Detailed Description

Abstract Strategy: one algorithm for choosing a worker from a pool.

Strategies only decide who gets the work. They know nothing about roles, approval or item state; roles are handled by the Chain of Responsibility, and the pool is built by the caller (e.g. ParticipantRegistry::getByRole).
