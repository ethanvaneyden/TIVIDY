Approved treatments get their reviewer recorded ("Batch"). Treatments the chain cannot approve are rejected.

◆ openWard()

int hospital::HospitalAdmissionCoordinator::openWard ( Container & ward)

Open a ward for treatment: Intake -> Active, then assign a doctor to every available treatment through the assignment engine.

Items can only be assigned in the Active phase, so assignment is done here, one treatment at a time, which lets a workload strategy balance the doctors.

Returns
the number of treatments that received a doctor.
Exceptions
InvalidTransitionif the ward is empty or not in Intake.
