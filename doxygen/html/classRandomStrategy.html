Detailed Description

Concrete Strategy: chooses a worker uniformly at random.

Needs no seed from the caller. The generator is seeded with a fixed constant inside the class, so runs are repeatable (useful for demos and tests). Change SEED to std::random_device{}() for different picks each run.
